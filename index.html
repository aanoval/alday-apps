Juragan Kos

Landlord tycoon · 3D simulation

A cosy 3D landlord tycoon set in an Indonesian alley. Inherit Emak's run-down kos, fix the rooms, take in tenants with their own stories, run the diner and the laundry next door, and grow into the juragan of the street.

Unfold Cafe

Idle café tycoon

An idle café tycoon built around one move: opening the screen. Folded, your iPhone Duo is the counter; unfold it and the 3D street appears, with your new café cutting its ribbon. Grow one café into a chain across 40 cities in 10 countries, with no ads, ever.

Toastello

Sandwich cooking game

A cosy cooking game about one thing done well: the perfect grilled sandwich. Build it, set the heat and close the press. There is no timer: open the lid when the cheese is through and the bread is golden.
