Support and FAQ

Answers to common questions about CribCount. If you do not find what you need, contact us.

Keeping score

How do I peg points?

Tap a number under a player's score. The keys go from 1 to 12; tap 13+ for a bigger hand. The front peg moves and the back peg stays where the player stood before, exactly as on a wooden board.

I tapped the wrong number.

Tap Undo under the board. It takes back the latest move, whoever made it, and you can undo as many moves as you need.

What is face to face?

With two players, the far player's keys are turned around so each of you reads your own side with the phone lying between you. Turn it off in Players and Rules (the people icon at the top left).

What do S and SS on the board mean?

They mark the skunk lines. In a 121-point game a loser who has not reached 91 is skunked, and one who has not reached 61 is double skunked. In a 61-point game the skunk line is 31. CribCount names the skunk when the game ends.

How do I change names or start again?

Names, the number of players and the game length are in Players and Rules. New under the board clears the pegs. A finished game is added to the history when you start the next one.

Does the app deal cards or play against me?

No. CribCount is a score keeper for a game you play with real cards. It has no computer opponent, no online play and no wagering.

Hand counter (Pro)

How do I count a hand?

Tap the spade icon. Pick a rank and then a suit for each of your four cards, then the starter. The total appears with each group: fifteens, pairs, runs, flush and nobs. Tap a card to change it, and tap Peg to add the total to a player.

Why is my flush not counted in the crib?

In the crib a flush only scores when all five cards, including the starter, are the same suit. Turn on "This is the crib" when you count it.

What is nobs?

One point for holding the jack of the same suit as the starter. A jack turned up as the starter is "his heels" (2 for the dealer), which you peg yourself.

CribCount Pro

What is free and what is Pro?

Two-player games to 121 on the walnut board are free, with undo. CribCount Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that adds the hand counter, match history and standings, three-player games, the 61-point game and the maple and slate boards.

Why does the Pro screen appear when I open the app?

The first launch shows what Pro adds, and it may appear again at most once a day until you buy. Tap Not Now or Continue with the Free Version to close it. It never appears once you own Pro.

How do I restore my purchase?

  1. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that bought CribCount Pro (Settings, then your name at the top).
  2. Open CribCount, open the More menu (the circled dots), then Settings & About.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

You are not charged again. If restore does not work, check your internet connection, confirm the Apple Account, restart the app and try again.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com while signed in with your Apple Account. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. The game in progress and the history are stored per device and do not sync. Deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.
