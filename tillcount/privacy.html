Privacy Policy

TillCount for iPhone and iPad, by Alday Inc. Effective date: November 11, 2026.

Short version: TillCount does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. TillCount has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not operate a server that receives data from the app.

Information stored on your device

The quantities, amounts, check notes, drawer names and saved counts you enter are kept only in the app's storage on your device so they are there the next time you open the app. We cannot see or access this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include the app's data in that backup under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to those backups.

Reports and shared summaries

If you use TillCount Pro to create a PDF count report or share a count as text, the file or text is generated on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to share, save or print it using the iOS share sheet, and only to the destination you choose.

Purchases

The optional TillCount Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account, payment card or billing details. Apple provides developers with aggregated sales reports that do not identify individual customers. Apple's handling of your purchase information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Tracking and advertising

TillCount does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier and shows no ads.

Children

TillCount is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Contacting support

If you contact us, we receive whatever you choose to include in the message. We use it only to reply to you and to resolve your request, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app were to begin collecting any data, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details would be updated before that version is released.
