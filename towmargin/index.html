TowMargin

A towing weight calculator for iPhone. Enter the ratings for your tow vehicle and trailer, add your passengers and cargo, and see whether each weight is within its rating and how many pounds of margin are left.

Support and FAQ Privacy Policy

What it checks

  • Payload, including tongue or pin weight
  • Maximum trailer (towing capacity) and trailer GVWR
  • Combined weight against GCWR
  • Hitch ratings for trailer weight and tongue or pin weight
  • Tongue or pin weight as a share of trailer weight

Free and Pro

Free: the complete weight check for one rig (one tow vehicle, one trailer and its load) with every check and margin shown. No ads.

TowMargin Pro: a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Adds unlimited saved rigs, itemized cargo lists for vehicle and trailer, and a PDF weight report you can export or share.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Estimates only. TowMargin calculates from the numbers you enter. It does not weigh anything and does not state that any setup is safe to tow. Confirm your ratings on the manufacturer's door-jamb stickers, trailer VIN label and owner's manuals, and weigh your loaded rig on a certified scale such as a CAT Scale.

For iPhone, iOS 17 or later. US units (pounds). Made by Alday Inc.
