Support and FAQ

Answers to common questions about TapeCount. If you do not find what you need, contact us.

Using the machine

Why does nothing print when I type a number?

TapeCount works like a desk adding machine. Type the number first, then press + to add it or − to subtract it. That prints the line. Press Total when the column is finished.

What is the difference between Sub and Total?

Sub prints the sum so far and keeps adding to it. Total prints the sum and starts a new one, so the next entry begins from zero. Both show how many items were added.

How do I multiply or divide?

Type the first number, press × or ÷, type the second number, then press + or − to print the result on the tape with its expression. Press = if you only want to see the result first.

What do CE, the arrow, ± and New do?

CE clears the number you are typing. The arrow deletes the last digit. ± changes the sign. New clears the tape after asking you to confirm.

How many decimals does it keep?

Up to four, and at least two are shown. Arithmetic is exact decimal arithmetic, not binary floating point, so cents add up correctly.

Why did the tape stop accepting lines?

The free tape holds 20 lines. Clear it to start again, or get TapeCount Pro for tapes of any length. The number you typed is kept while you decide.

TapeCount Pro

What is free and what is Pro?

The full adding machine with one 20-line tape is free. TapeCount Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that adds tapes of any length, editing and deleting lines, saved tapes, the tax key and line notes, and sharing or printing the tape.

How do I fix a wrong line? (Pro)

Tap the line on the tape. Change the amount or the sign, add a note, or delete the line, then Save. Every subtotal and total below it is recalculated.

How does the Tax key work? (Pro)

Set your rate once in Settings & About. The Tax key then adds that percent of the running total as its own line, rounded to the cent.

Why does the Pro screen appear when I open the app?

The first launch shows what Pro adds, and it may appear again at most once a day until you buy. Tap Not Now or Continue with the Free Version to close it. It never appears once you own Pro.

How do I restore my purchase?

  1. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that bought TapeCount Pro (Settings, then your name at the top).
  2. Open TapeCount, open the More menu (the circled dots), then Settings & About.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

You are not charged again. If restore does not work, check your internet connection, confirm the Apple Account, restart the app and try again.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com while signed in with your Apple Account. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Tapes are stored per device and do not sync. Deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.
