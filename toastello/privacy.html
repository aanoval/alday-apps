Privacy Policy

Toastello for iPhone, by Alday Inc. Effective date: November 13, 2026.

Short version: Toastello collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no server that receives anything from the game. Your game is saved on your device.

Information we collect

None. Toastello has no account or sign-in, contains no advertising or analytics software, does not track you, and does not send your game data anywhere.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (days, stars, tips, butter, decor, saved sandwiches and their names) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are kept only in the game's storage on your device. We cannot see or access them. Deleting the game deletes them, including unused butter.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include the game's data in that backup under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to those backups.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to them.

Sharing

"Share Toastello" in Settings opens the iPhone's own share sheet with a link to the game. What you send and to whom is between you and the app you choose; the game learns nothing about it.

Children

Toastello collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Support page.
