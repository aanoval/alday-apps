Support and FAQ

Answers to common questions about TillCount. If you do not find what you need, contact us.

Counting a drawer

How do I count?

Tap the box next to a bill or coin and type how many you have, or use the plus and minus buttons. The arrows above the keyboard jump to the next or previous denomination, so you can count the whole drawer without leaving the keyboard. The total updates as you type.

Can I count smallest to largest?

Yes. Open the More menu (the circled dots) and choose Smallest First, or change Denomination order in Settings & About.

How do I add checks or other items?

In Checks & Other, tap Add Check or Other, type a note and the amount. These lines count toward the drawer total and always go to the deposit.

Over, short and the deposit

What do Balanced, Over and Short mean?

Enter the amount your register report says should be in the drawer under Expected in drawer. TillCount subtracts it from what you counted. Zero is Balanced; more than expected is Over; less is Short.

What is the starting float?

The float (also called the bank or the till) is the cash you leave in the drawer so the next shift can make change, often $100 to $300. Enter it once under Starting float. TillCount shows how much stays and how much is your deposit. The float is kept when you clear the count.

How does the deposit split choose what to pull? (Pro)

It keeps your change. Loose coins, rolls and small bills stay in the drawer up to the float amount, and large bills go to the bank first. If the pieces in the drawer cannot make the float exactly, the app leaves the closest amount below it and tells you the difference.

My total looks wrong. What should I check?

  • A coin roll is entered under Coin Rolls, not as loose coins.
  • Each quantity is a number of pieces, not a dollar amount: three $20 bills is 3, not 60.
  • Scroll the whole list for a quantity left over from an earlier count, or use Clear Count and start again.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Counting works offline. A connection is needed only when you buy or restore TillCount Pro, because the App Store handles that.

Does it support other currencies?

Not at this time. TillCount counts US dollars.

How does it look on iPhone Duo?

Folded, it is one list like on any iPhone. Unfolded, and on iPad, the totals and the counting list are shown as two panes.

TillCount Pro

What is free and what is Pro?

Counting one drawer is free, with over and short and the deposit amount. TillCount Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that adds unlimited drawers, a history of saved counts, the piece-by-piece deposit split and a PDF count report.

How do I restore my purchase?

  1. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account that bought TillCount Pro (Settings, then your name at the top).
  2. Open TillCount, open the More menu, then Settings & About.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

You are not charged again. If restore does not work, check your internet connection, confirm the Apple Account, restart the app and try again.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com while signed in with your Apple Account. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Does Pro work on my other devices?

Yes, on any iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account; use Restore Purchases. Drawers and history are stored per device and do not sync.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your drawers and history. See the privacy policy.
