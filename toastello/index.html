About the game

About Toastello

Toastello is a sandwich maker and grilled cheese cooking game for iPhone by the independent studio Alday Inc. Regulars walk up and order: this bread, this cheese, these fillings, golden please. You build the sandwich, choose the heat and close the panini press.

There is no timer. Open too early and the cheese is still firm; too late and it runs out or the bread burns. It is a calm, tactile cooking game where sound and steam tell you when the toastie is ready.

The first days are free and the Full Game is a single purchase. No ads, no subscription, ten languages.

FAQ

Questions

Is Toastello free?

The first four days are free. The Full Game is one purchase that opens all 40 days and every mode. There are no ads and no subscription.

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

No. On every iPhone you swipe the lid down and up. On iPhone Duo a food critic now and then asks for the Real Press, where you fold the phone to grill.

Which languages are supported?

English, Bahasa Indonesia, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Korean and Russian.
