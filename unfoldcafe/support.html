Support and FAQ

Answers to common questions about Unfold Cafe. If you do not find what you need, contact us.

Playing

How do I open a café?

On the counter, tap to serve customers until you can afford the next location, then tap Sign lease. Now open the screen: unfold your iPhone Duo, or on any other iPhone swipe up on the bar at the bottom. The café opens the moment the board appears.

I do not have an iPhone Duo. Can I play?

Yes. Swipe up on the bar at the bottom of the counter to open the city board, and swipe down on the bar at the top to return. Tapping the bar works too.

Why can I not sign a lease on the board?

Leases are signed on the counter and cafés open on the board. Going from one to the other is the game.

What happens while I am away?

Your cafés keep earning for up to 2 hours. The Loyalty App item in the shop raises that to 8 hours.

What are beans?

Beans buy permanent items and espresso shots (2× income for 10 minutes) in the shop. You earn them at every grand opening, from the day's goals, from VIP customers and for every country you complete, or you can buy a pack.

How do combos work?

Tap the counter quickly: taps less than a second apart build a combo. At 10, 25, 50 and 100 taps every cup is worth 1.5×, 2×, 3× and 5×. Now and then a cup is a perfect pour, worth 5× on its own.

What is the VIP customer?

A golden customer crosses the screen every minute or so. Tap them before they leave for a big tip in coins, or sometimes beans.

How do I turn off the music or the sound?

Settings (the gear button), then Sound: separate switches for sound effects, music and vibration. The game also follows the silent switch, and its music stays quiet while another app plays music.

Purchases

Does the game have ads?

No. Unfold Cafe has no ads of any kind.

What does Double Income do?

It is a one-time purchase: every café's income and every cup you serve earn twice as much, for good. A "2× forever" badge appears under your coins.

I bought Double Income but the badge is missing

Open the shop (the green beans button) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

I bought beans and they did not arrive

Close the game completely and open it again while online; an interrupted purchase is delivered at the next launch. If the purchase shows as waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), the beans arrive when it is approved.

Do bean packs come back with Restore Purchases?

No. Bean packs are used up when spent, so there is nothing to restore. Double Income is restored.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com while signed in with your Apple Account. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Where is my game saved?

Only on your device. There is no account and progress does not sync between devices. Deleting the game deletes your progress, including unused beans. See the privacy policy.
