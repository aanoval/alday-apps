Juragan Kos

A boarding-house landlord tycoon in 3D for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad, by Alday Inc.

From Emak's three worn rooms to the busiest kos in Gang Melati. Repair and build rooms, take in tenants with their own needs and night stories, run the diner and the laundry next door, hire staff and buy plot after plot along the street.

How it plays

  • Collect rent at each door, set the price, repair, upgrade and add Wi-Fi, AC and a water heater.
  • Thirty kinds of tenants, each with a budget, needs and a story told at night.
  • Cook at the diner with a few taps, wash and fold at the laundry.
  • Hire five kinds of staff, finish 40 missions and three daily challenges, grow to six plots of three floors.
  • On an unfolded iPhone Duo or an iPad the street simply shows wider.

Price

Free to play with ads from Google AdMob and optional in-app purchases. Juragan Premium or Remove Ads turns the ads off; staff, plots and rooms are never sold for real money.
