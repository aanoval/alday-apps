Support and FAQ

Answers to common questions about Juragan Kos. If you do not find what you need, contact us.

Playing

How do I get started?

Emak leaves you a three-room kos with a little cash. Tap a room door to look inside and repair it, take in a tenant at the front desk (the people waiting at the desk in front of the first building), and collect rent at each door every morning.

How do I move around the street?

Drag on the lower part of the street for a joystick that walks the juragan, through a plot's gate into its yard and up to the doors (standing at a ground-floor door shows a Look inside button). Drag higher or twist two fingers to turn the view, pinch or tap Zoom to come closer or see more, and tap a door, a shop or a person to walk there. On an unfolded iPhone Duo or on an iPad you simply see more of the street at once.

Why did a tenant move out?

Tenants leave after two unhappy days or when rent stays unpaid too long. Keep rooms repaired, give them what they need (Wi-Fi, AC, a quiet neighbour), handle their requests and keep the rent fair.

When does the day end?

From 19:00 game time you can sleep (the Sleep button, or at home) to close the day: staff are paid, the recap shows your profit and the game is saved. At midnight the day closes by itself.

How do I get more plots and floors?

Open Build in the bottom bar. A plot needs the reputation level shown and the money; floors need money. Plots, rooms and staff are never sold for real money.

How do I turn off the music or the sound?

Settings (the gear button), then Sound: separate switches for sound effects, music, street sounds and vibration.

Purchases and ads

Does the game have ads?

Yes, from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward (2x rent today, a VIP tenant, a faster renovation, double offline rent), sometimes an ad after the end-of-day recap, an ad when you come back to the game, and a banner on the recap. Remove Ads or Juragan Premium turns off all but the ads you choose to watch; with Juragan Premium the rewards come without any ad.

Which purchases are there?

Juragan Sultan Bundle, Juragan Premium, the Starter Juragan Pack (first week), Double Rent, Permanent Offline 2x, Fast Builder, the Juragan Style Pack, Remove Ads, and three cash packs. All are in the Shop (the bag button).

I bought something but it is not there

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom, signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. An interrupted cash pack arrives the next time the game opens.

Do cash packs come back with Restore Purchases?

No. In-game cash is used up when spent. Every other purchase is restored. The Starter Pack's cash is paid once per saved game.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com while signed in with your Apple Account. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Where is my game saved?

Only on your device. There is no account and progress does not sync between devices. Deleting the game deletes your progress. See the privacy policy.
