CribCount

A cribbage pegboard for iPhone and iPad. Play with your own cards and let the phone keep the score: tap the points, the pegs move, and the board calls the skunk.

Support and FAQ Privacy Policy

What it does

  • A full 121-point board with a front and a back peg for each player
  • One-tap pegging keys for each player, and undo for the last move
  • Face-to-face seating: the far player's keys are turned around
  • Skunk and double skunk lines marked on the board and called at the end
  • With Pro: a hand counter for fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes and nobs
  • With Pro: match history with wins, losses and skunks
  • With Pro: three players, the 61-point game, and maple and slate boards

Free and Pro

Free: two-player games to 121 on the walnut board, with undo. No ads, no time limit.

CribCount Pro: a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Adds the hand counter, match history and standings, three-player games, the 61-point game and two more boards.

Built for iPhone Duo and iPad too

On a regular iPhone the board sits between the two players' keys. On the unfolded iPhone Duo and on iPad the board and the keys are shown side by side as two panes.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.

For iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. Made by Alday Inc. CribCount is a score keeper, not a card game, and involves no wagering.
