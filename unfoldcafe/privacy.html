Privacy Policy

Unfold Cafe for iPhone, by Alday Inc. Effective date: November 12, 2026.

Short version: Unfold Cafe collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no server that receives anything from the game. Your game is saved on your device.

Information we collect

None. Unfold Cafe has no account or sign-in, contains no advertising or analytics software, does not track you, and does not send your game data anywhere.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, beans, cafés, items) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are kept only in the game's storage on your device. We cannot see or access them. Deleting the game deletes them, including unused beans.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include the game's data in that backup under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to those backups.

Maps

The map where you pick your cities uses Apple Maps (MapKit). Apple delivers the map tiles; the game does not ask for your location. Apple's privacy policy applies to map requests.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to them.

Children

Unfold Cafe collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Support page.
