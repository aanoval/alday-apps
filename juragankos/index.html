About the game

About Juragan Kos

Juragan Kos is a landlord tycoon and boarding house simulator for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, made by the independent studio Alday Inc. You manage a kos (an Indonesian boarding house) in Gang Melati: you rent rooms to tenants, keep them happy and expand the building floor by floor and plot by plot.

It plays like a cosy real estate and apartment tycoon with the daily rhythm of a life simulator: mornings at the market, rent at every door, the diner at lunch, the laundry in the afternoon and night stories before you sleep. Thirty tenant types, 40 missions and three daily challenges keep every day different.

The game is free to play, works offline and is available in ten languages. Staff, plots and rooms are never sold for real money; Juragan Premium removes the ads.

FAQ

Questions

Is Juragan Kos free?

Yes. Juragan Kos is free to download and play. It shows optional ads and offers in-app purchases; Juragan Premium or Remove Ads turns the ads off. Rooms, staff and plots are earned by playing, never sold for real money.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. On an unfolded iPhone Duo and on iPad the 3D street shows wider.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game is saved on your device and plays without a connection; rent keeps piling up while you are away.

Which languages are supported?

English, Bahasa Indonesia, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Korean and Russian.

What is a kos?

A kos (kos-kosan) is an Indonesian boarding house: a building of rented rooms, often for students and young workers. In the game you are the juragan, the landlord.
