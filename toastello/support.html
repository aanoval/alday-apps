Support and FAQ

Answers to common questions about Toastello. If you do not find what you need, contact us.

Playing

How do I cook a sandwich?

Open the press: unfold your iPhone Duo, or on any other iPhone swipe up on the bar at the bottom. Read the order, tap the bread, what goes on the outside, the cheese and the fillings, then tap Onto the press. Close the press to grill, open it when you think it is ready, and tap Serve.

I do not have an iPhone Duo. Can I play?

Yes. Swipe down on the bar at the top to close the lid and up on the bar at the bottom to open it, or use the Close the lid button. Tapping the bars works too.

Where is the timer?

There is none, on purpose. The sizzle gets quieter as the bread dries, the steam thins, and your iPhone gives a short pulse when the cheese has melted. You can open the lid to look, but an open press cooks slowly. With the Full Game, or with a cook's tip token, the order card shows the best heat and time.

Why did the cheese run out?

The sandwich stayed closed too long for that cheese. Use higher heat when the customer wants dark bread, so the bread is done before the cheese runs. A better press (Kitchen, the brush button) gives a little more room.

What do stars do?

Every sandwich earns up to five stars: for the recipe, the toast and the cheese. Three stars on average pass a day and open the next one. You can cook any order again; the best result counts.

What are tips and butter?

Tips come with every sandwich and buy kitchen decor, press levels and looks. They are never sold. Butter buys second chances, cook's tips and extra tries at the daily special. You earn butter by passing days, from regulars and from the daily special, or you can buy a pack.

How do I turn off the music or the sound?

Settings (the gear button), then Sound: separate switches for sound effects, music and vibration. The game also follows the silent switch, and stays quiet while another app plays music.

Purchases

Does the game have ads?

No. Toastello has no ads of any kind, and no subscription.

What does the Full Game open?

Days 5 to 40, the Test Kitchen, the two-player Grill-off, the two-minute Rush and the cook's tip on every order. It is a one-time purchase. Recipe packs are separate purchases.

I bought the Full Game but the days are still locked

Open the shop (the bag button) and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

I bought butter and it did not arrive

Close the game completely and open it again while online; an interrupted purchase is delivered at the next launch. If the purchase shows as waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), the butter arrives when it is approved.

Do butter packs come back with Restore Purchases?

No. Butter is used up when spent, so there is nothing to restore. The Full Game, the Starter Pack's copper press and the recipe packs are restored. The Starter Pack's butter and second chances are paid out once per saved game.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com while signed in with your Apple Account. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Where is my game saved?

Only on your device. There is no account and progress does not sync between devices. Deleting the game deletes your progress, including unused butter. See the privacy policy.

Contact
