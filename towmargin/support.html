Support and FAQ

Answers to common questions about TowMargin and the weight ratings it uses. If you do not find what you need, contact us.

Estimates only. TowMargin calculates from the numbers you enter. It does not weigh anything and does not state that any setup is safe to tow. Confirm your ratings on the manufacturer's door-jamb stickers, trailer VIN label and owner's manuals, and weigh your loaded rig on a certified scale such as a CAT Scale.

What the ratings mean

GVWR (Gross Vehicle Weight Rating)
The most a single vehicle is rated to weigh when fully loaded: the vehicle itself, fuel, passengers, cargo and, for a tow vehicle, the tongue or pin weight pressing down on its hitch. Your truck has a GVWR and your trailer has its own separate GVWR.
Payload
How much weight you can add to the tow vehicle: people, pets, cargo, bed accessories, the hitch hardware and the trailer's tongue or pin weight. It is roughly GVWR minus the weight of the vehicle as it left the factory. Options and accessories added later reduce it. Payload is often the first rating a truck reaches when towing a travel trailer or fifth wheel.
GCWR (Gross Combined Weight Rating)
The most the loaded tow vehicle and the loaded trailer are rated to weigh together.
Maximum trailer weight (towing capacity)
The heaviest loaded trailer the manufacturer rates the vehicle to pull, usually published for a lightly loaded vehicle. Being under this number does not mean you are under payload, GVWR or GCWR, which is why TowMargin checks each one.
Tongue weight (conventional trailers)
The downward force the trailer coupler puts on the hitch ball. It counts against the tow vehicle's payload and the hitch rating. A commonly recommended range is about 10 to 15 percent of the loaded trailer weight; too little is associated with trailer sway. Follow your trailer and hitch manufacturer's guidance.
Pin weight (fifth wheel and gooseneck)
The same idea for a hitch mounted in the truck bed. It is typically higher, commonly about 15 to 25 percent of the loaded trailer weight, and it also counts against payload and the rear axle.
GAWR (Gross Axle Weight Rating)
The most each axle is rated to carry. There is a front GAWR and a rear GAWR. Tongue or pin weight and bed cargo load mainly the rear axle, so the rear GAWR is the one most often approached when towing.
Hitch ratings
The receiver, ball mount and ball each carry a maximum trailer weight and maximum tongue weight, sometimes with separate numbers for weight-carrying and weight-distributing use. The lowest-rated component sets the limit.
Dry weight / UVW versus loaded weight
A trailer's dry or unloaded vehicle weight (UVW) excludes water, propane, batteries in some cases, and your gear. Use the loaded weight, ideally from a scale. If you do not have one yet, the trailer's GVWR is a conservative stand-in.

Where to find the numbers

NumberWhere to look
PayloadThe yellow-and-white Tire and Loading Information sticker on the driver's door edge or door jamb. Look for the line that begins "The combined weight of occupants and cargo should never exceed" followed by a weight in lb. This number is specific to your vehicle as built.
GVWR, front and rear GAWRThe safety certification label, usually white or silver, on the driver's door jamb or door edge near the latch. It also lists the VIN, build date and tire sizes.
GCWR and maximum trailer weightUsually not on the door jamb. Check the towing section of the owner's manual or the manufacturer's towing guide for your model year, engine, axle ratio, cab and drivetrain. Some newer trucks have a separate trailering information label on the door jamb.
Hitch ratingsThe label or stamping on the receiver itself, plus the ratings stamped on the ball mount and ball.
Trailer GVWR and GAWRThe VIN / weight label on the trailer, typically on the front left (driver side) exterior corner or on the frame near the tongue. RVs also have a cargo carrying capacity label, usually inside a cabinet door or by the entry door.
Actual weightsA certified scale. At a CAT Scale, weigh the truck and trailer together, then the truck alone, to get steer axle, drive axle, trailer axle and combined weights.

Label designs and locations vary by manufacturer and model year. If a label is missing or unreadable, ask a dealer for the ratings for your VIN.

Using the app

What do "Within ratings", "Near limit" and "Over rating" mean?

For each check, TowMargin compares your estimated weight with the rating you entered. "Within ratings" shows how many pounds remain; "Near limit" means you are at 90 percent of the rating or more; "Over rating" shows how many pounds you exceed it by. "Not evaluated" means a number the check needs is still blank. TowMargin does not check axle ratings (GAWR) or tires, which need a scale reading. No result is a statement that a setup is safe or unsafe to tow. It is arithmetic on your inputs.

My result looks wrong. What should I check?

  • Payload should come from the yellow sticker on your own vehicle, not from a brochure or website.
  • Enter the trailer's dry weight and everything you add to it (water, propane, gear) as trailer cargo, so the app works with the loaded weight, not the dry weight.
  • Include everyone and everything in the truck: passengers, pets, tools, bed cover, the hitch itself, a full cooler.
  • All values are in pounds (lb).

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All calculations run on your iPhone. A connection is needed only when you buy or restore TowMargin Pro, because the App Store handles that.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your saved rigs. See the privacy policy.

Does it support kilograms?

Not at this time. TowMargin uses US pounds, matching US vehicle and trailer labels.

TowMargin Pro

What is free and what is Pro?

The complete weight check for one rig is free, with every check and margin shown. TowMargin Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that adds unlimited saved rigs, itemized cargo lists for the vehicle and trailer, and PDF weight report export.

How do I restore my purchase?

  1. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account that bought TowMargin Pro (Settings, then your name at the top).
  2. Open TowMargin and go to the TowMargin Pro screen.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

You are not charged again. If you tap Buy for something you already own on that Apple Account, the App Store also restores it at no charge. If restore does not work, check your internet connection, confirm the Apple Account, restart the app and try again.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com while signed in with your Apple Account. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Does Pro work on my other devices?

Yes, on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account; use Restore Purchases. Saved rigs are stored per device and do not sync.
