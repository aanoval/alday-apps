About the app

About CribCount

CribCount is a cribbage board and score keeper app for iPhone and iPad by Alday Inc, for playing cribbage with a real deck of cards anywhere.

Related: cribbage board, cribbage scorer, cribbage pegboard, cribbage hand counter, crib score keeper, card game scoreboard.
