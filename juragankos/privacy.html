Privacy Policy

Juragan Kos for iPhone and iPad, by Alday Inc. Effective date: November 13, 2026.

Short version: Juragan Kos has no account and we run no server that receives your data. Your game is saved on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to deliver and measure those ads, as described below.

Information we collect ourselves

None. Juragan Kos has no account or sign-in, and we do not operate a server that receives data from the game.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (your kos, rooms, tenants, cash, missions and settings) is kept only in the game's storage on your device. We cannot see or access it. Deleting the game deletes it, including unspent in-game cash.

If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include the game's data in that backup under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to those backups.

Advertising

Juragan Kos shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To deliver, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived;
  • a device identifier, such as the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking;
  • which ads you saw and whether you tapped them, and when the game was opened;
  • crash and performance information about the ad software.

This data goes from your device to Google and its advertising partners. It does not pass through us and we do not receive it in a form that identifies you. How Google uses it is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking permission. The first time you open the game, iOS asks whether Juragan Kos may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are less personalized. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The game plays the same either way.
  • Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland. You are asked for consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • US state privacy laws. Where they apply, the same Settings row lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising.
  • Remove Ads and Juragan Premium. Either one-time purchase turns off banner ads, ads between screens and ads when you return to the game. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available; if you never start one, no such ad is shown.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to them.

Children

Juragan Kos is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
