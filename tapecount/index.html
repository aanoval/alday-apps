TapeCount

An adding machine with a paper tape for iPhone and iPad. Key in a column of figures the 10-key way, see every entry printed on the tape, and check it line by line before you trust the total.

Support and FAQ Privacy Policy

What it does

  • Adding machine entry: type a number, press + or −, and it prints on the tape
  • Subtotal and Total keys with an item count, and a running total always in view
  • Multiply and divide, with the expression printed beside the result
  • Exact decimal arithmetic, so 0.10 + 0.20 is 0.30
  • With Pro: tapes of any length, edit or delete any line, saved tapes, a tax key, line notes, and PDF or text sharing

Free and Pro

Free: the full adding machine with one tape of up to 20 lines. No ads, no time limit.

TapeCount Pro: a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Adds tapes of any length, editing and deleting lines with automatic recalculation, as many saved tapes as you need, the tax key and line notes, and sharing or printing the tape.

Built for iPhone Duo and iPad too

On a regular iPhone the tape is above the keys. On the unfolded iPhone Duo and on iPad the tape and the keypad are shown side by side as two panes.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
