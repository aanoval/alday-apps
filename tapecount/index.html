About the app

About TapeCount

TapeCount is an adding machine and 10-key tape calculator for iPhone and iPad by Alday Inc, made for bookkeepers, treasurers and anyone reconciling receipts.

Related: adding machine, 10 key calculator, tape calculator, printing calculator, bookkeeping calculator, checkbook calculator.

The full adding machine with one tape of up to 20 lines is free with no ads. Pro is a one-time purchase.
